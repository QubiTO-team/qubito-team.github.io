Activities

International hackathons, events and seminars we organise at the Politecnico, and the occasions where we explain quantum computing to people who meet it for the first time.

Upcoming

  1. 11–13 December 2026 Politecnico di Torino

    QubiTO Hackathon 2026

    Company talks and a 24-hour quantum computing hackathon at the Politecnico, organised by QubiTO.

2026

  1. June–July 2026 Politecnico di Torino

    QubiTO Quantum Seminars

    Three seminars on the foundations of quantum mechanics, with Marco Giovanelli, Marco Genovese and Davide Romano.

  2. 8–10 May 2026 ETH Zurich

    ETH Quantum Hackathon 2026 in Zurich

    3rd place and honourable mention in the QuEra challenge

    Two challenges, by QuEra and Zurich Instruments: fault-tolerant circuit synthesis and autonomous qubit calibration.

2025

  1. December 2025 Madrid

    IQM Quantum Hack in Madrid

    2nd place in the Indra challenge

    24 hours of quantum computing on the Indra and Santander challenges, with a modified Monte Carlo Tree Search.

  2. November 2025 TUM, Garching (Munich)

    Pushquantum Hackathon at TUM in Munich

    An error correction strategy for very limited hardware, comparing standard algorithms, optimisation models and a solution of our own.

  3. 12–14 November 2025 Politecnico di Torino

    QubiTO Quantum Days

    Three days at the Politecnico for the centenary of quantum mechanics, with Riccardo Adami, Scott Aaronson and a workshop on the Lagrange quantum computer.

  4. October 2025 CERN, Geneva

    GenQ Global Hackathon Series in Geneva

    2nd place and Innovation Award (medical imaging)

    Quantum machine learning at CERN on two problems: medical image classification and air quality forecasting.

  5. May 2025 ETH Zurich

    ETH Quantum Hackathon 2025 in Zurich

    Two teams on the QCentroid (portfolio optimization) and QuEra (gate parallelization) challenges.

  6. 28–29 March 2025 Politecnico di Torino, R rooms

    Orientation Fair 2025

    Second participation at the Orientation Fair, meeting new students interested in quantum physics.

  7. 14 March 2025

    Pi Day

    Celebrating Pi Day with a quantum perspective, exploring the role of π in theoretical physics and quantum mechanics.

2024

  1. 2024 TUM, Garching (Munich)

    Pushquantum Hackathon 2024

    Participation in the international hackathon at the TUM Entrepreneurship Research Institute in Munich.

  2. November 2024 Politecnico di Torino

    Internal Hackathon 2024

    Second internal hackathon, on quantum optimization and quantum machine learning, with original projects and new application horizons.

  3. 2024 Toolbox Coworking, Turin

    DevFest Alps 2024

    Second participation at Google DevFest Alps with a new talk, sharing our passion for quantum computing.

  4. 2024 ETH Zurich

    ETH Quantum Hackathon 2024

    Our first international hackathon at ETH Zurich: 24 hours of intense work on challenges proposed by startups and companies.

  5. 13 April 2024 Politecnico di Torino

    Orientation Fair 2024

    During Politecnico's annual Orientation Fair, we introduced ourselves to curious and quantum-interested prospective students.

2023

  1. 2023 Room 1, Politecnico di Torino

    Exploring Quantum Opportunities

    Our first major outreach event, with BeQuantum: over 200 people in the room to talk about careers in quantum technologies.

  2. 2023 Toolbox Coworking, Turin

    DevFest Alps 2023

    On stage at Google DevFest Alps, we presented our work on Grover's algorithm and quantum oracles.

  3. November 2023 Politecnico di Torino

    Internal Hackathon 2023

    An internal challenge on Grover's algorithm: mixed teams, working on their own and competing against each other, as in a real hackathon.

Italy's first student team in quantum computing.
